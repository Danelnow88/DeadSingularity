# CORREDOR CÓSMICO ÁMBAR — concepto v1

15/10/2026. Primera propuesta visual, pendiente de aprobación del usuario.
Archivo: previews/concepts/corredor-cosmico-ambar-v1.png.

## Decisión de diseño

Nuevo diseño desde cero para el Corredor, no una integración del remodel-v1
mineral rechazado. Comparte con Espíritu y Escopuras aprobados la materia
negra orgánica, pliegues curvos, reflejos plata y textura estelar contenida.
Dos ojos grandes directamente incrustados en una masa compacta y barrida hacia
atrás; acentos ámbar acordes a la identidad cálida del Corredor, no una armadura
dorada. Sin cabeza/torso humano, brazos, manos, patas, armas ni rotores.

La silueta apunta a lectura de flanqueador/embestida mediante el volumen delantero
y los pliegues posteriores. Se mantiene el diseño dentro del lenguaje cósmico;
la valoración de si expresa suficiente velocidad corresponde a la revisión visual.
No se modifica la mecánica, tamaño, velocidad, daño, spawns ni hitbox actuales.

## Estado de producción y límites

Generado con la habilidad imagegen y herramienta integrada, con cinco imágenes
de referencia: los dos conceptos aprobados y las tres referencias artísticas
del usuario. No se utilizó CLI ni se redibujaron los enemigos integrados.

Imagen estática con ojos pintados: todavía NO sigue al jugador ni anticipa su
embestida. No se creó CMD ni se agregó a la partida/catálogo canónico. Si se
aprueba, preparar una prueba animada independiente con miradas y piezas acotadas,
comparar a tamaño de combate y medir bajo la misma carga antes de integrar.

Revisión visual inicial: dos ojos visibles, textura negra/plata con luces ámbar,
formas orgánicas sin anatomía humanoide, silueta completa sobre alpha. Tiene
microdetalle: no prometer que será visible íntegramente en tamaño pequeño ni
agrandar ciegamente al enemigo para compensarlo. La prueba animada deberá
validar lectura y encaje de escala con los otros comunes.
No se atribuye ahorro de rendimiento a esta ilustración.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE isolated 2D enemy concept sprite for DEADSINGULARITY, a fast flanking and short-dash enemy called CORREDOR. New creature design, not an edit or a collage.

Input references: Images 1 and 2 are the APPROVED Espiritu and Escopuras creatures. Match their exact family of glossy cosmic-black organic material, curved interwoven living folds, restrained silver edge reflections, pale biological eyes and embedded star texture; do NOT copy either silhouette. Images 3, 4 and 5 are secondary material/mood/organic expression references only. NEVER copy their humanoid anatomy, weapons, wings, hands, legs, standing posture or composition.

Subject and silhouette: a small, unmistakably LIVING floating pursuit organism, compact and streamlined, roughly one and a half times as long as wide. A low asymmetric swept teardrop of tightly folded living darkness, oriented diagonally forward. A thick smoothly rounded leading mass contains TWO large watchful eyes directly embedded side by side, slightly staggered, without any separate head, neck or torso. Its body narrows into two short overlapping backward-swept curved folds, with one deep crescent-shaped notch along the rear edge. These are attached folds of the organism, not arms, fins, wings or tentacles. A few broad hooked edges suggest a creature compressed like a spring, ready to dart around the player; avoid a rigid spearhead, arrow icon, ship, shield or geometric rock. Distinct silhouette from the round squat Escopuras and the single-eye curled Espiritu. No snout, mouth or teeth; let the eyes and compressed living folds establish its personality.

Materials and palette: same rich, intricately painted black cosmic tissue and woven silver-edged folds as approved references. Sparse warm AMBER stars and short nebular seams contained INSIDE the black material distinguish the Corredor from red Espiritu and green Escopuras. Amber is only the internal light accent, not a gold armor shell or yellow wash. White/silver stars remain white/silver. Large dark irises in smoky grey-white sclera, small coherent white catchlights, eyes large enough to read in a small combat sprite. Focus detail on broad folds and light-catching edges rather than thousands of tiny spikes. Real richness like the approved reference sprites, NOT a simplified vector cartoon or flat logo.

Composition: one entire creature centered, near-frontal/top-down token view suitable for a 2D battlefield, no dramatic camera perspective, clear readable eye-bearing front and trailing rear. Transparent margin of about 10% on all sides; nothing cropped. Genuine alpha transparency outside the silhouette. No floor, backdrop, cast shadow, aura, floating particles, smoke trail, panel, labels, text or watermark.
Production intent: still concept for approval, later two or three cached cutout parts plus eye movement, not a constantly rebuilt particle creature. Maintain beautiful material and a compact readable silhouette without making it a boss. NO humanoids, limbs, paws, claws, hands, feet, fish, insects, birds, mecha, rotors, armor plates, metal robot or crystalline polygon shells.
