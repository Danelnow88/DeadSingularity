## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Make ONE new game-creature concept on a genuinely transparent background, not a scene, not a collage.
Input image 1 is a MATERIAL/MOOD reference ONLY: living glossy black cosmic substance, dark starfield trapped inside, sharp silver glints and sparse crimson embers. Do NOT reproduce its human anatomy, wings, halo, weapons, face or composition.
Input image 2 is a GRAPHIC/EXPRESSION reference ONLY: eerie ink-dark silhouette, expressive round eyes and unsettling tapering edges. Do NOT reproduce its human body, arms, hands, legs, teeth or grin.
Subject: ESPIRITU, a SMALL floating phase creature in a 2D space roguelite. Entire organism is ONE compact, asymmetric coiled knot of living darkness, with ONE very large round watchful eye embedded directly in its mass. The eye IS the creature's center, not a head sitting on a body. Its whole silhouette resembles a curled comma or a folded raindrop turned sideways: rounded thick eye-bearing lobe, two broad curved interlocking shadow folds, one short tapering wake of torn ink. A few unequal short needle-like edges grow from these folds, NOT a radial sunburst. No separated limbs or appendages that could read as arms, no feet, no humanoid gesture or posture. No upper/lower human body hierarchy.
Art direction: gorgeous hand-painted dark fantasy game art with strong readable broad shapes, tangible glossy liquid shadow, NOT stone armor, rock plates, faceted crystals, jewelry, spaceship, fish or metal robot. An irregular internal cosmic sheen visible only INSIDE the black body, with a few small ruby-red embers, restrained muted jade/cool silver highlights tracing select edges. Silhouette must remain readable on a near-black game field without surrounding fog, bloom, a large glow or floating particles. The single eye is large and expressive: smoky pale grey sclera, deep black iris and tiny pale jade catchlight, subtly unsettling, not cute, not angry eyebrows. No mouth, smile, teeth, fangs or skull. Broad features readable when scaled to 45-60 pixels tall; avoid microtexture and excessive spines. This is a creature sprite, not a portrait or boss.
Composition: single isolated complete compact creature centered, front/top-down token-like view, no floor, no shadow under it, 10 percent transparent margin all around, no text. No humanoid anatomy under any circumstances. Genuine alpha background.
