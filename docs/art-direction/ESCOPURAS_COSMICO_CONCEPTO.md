## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE isolated transparent concept sprite for ESCOPURAS, a small ranged enemy in a 2D cosmic roguelite, not a scene or concept sheet.
Input image roles: Image 1 is the APPROVED ESPIRITU creature and defines material/finish only: glossy living black cosmic substance, contained star glints, sparse internal ruby-red embers and sharp selective silvery highlights. Do NOT reuse its single eye, curled comma shape or silhouette. Images 2, 3 and 4 are GLOBAL MOOD/ART references only: cosmic black living matter, eerie expressive eyes and unsettling organic folds. Do NOT copy any humanoid anatomy or giant boss silhouette from them.
NEW creature from scratch: a compact floating living pressure organism, a broad squat irregular ovoid body made of three thick softly pleated organic folds that wrap diagonally around one visibly compressible central pressure bladder. Its shape is asymmetric and coherent, not a pile of random thorns. At its lower-front edge grows ONE short thick fleshy siphon, pointing slightly downward and toward the viewer; the open oval discharge aperture is unmistakably part of a living organism, not a gun barrel or mask. The silhouette must communicate suction/pressure/spitting rather than clawing or slashing.
FOUR watchful biological eyes, different but close sizes, embedded directly along an uneven upper arc of the body: pale grey-blue sclera, glossy dark irises, tiny cold icy-blue glints. Eyes should remain expressive and readable when small. There is no separate head: the entire hovering mass IS the creature. No head on neck, no torso, shoulders, waist, arms, legs, hands, feet, wings or humanoid stance. No appendages that could resemble bent human limbs. No humanoids under any circumstances.
Material: tangible deep ink-black organic skin with broad smooth wet highlights over large soft folds, with a subtle dark starfield visible IN the substance, a FEW red ember threads, and restrained cool silver-blue edge accents. Let image 4 inform the organic membrane folds without copying its multiple mouths/tentacles. Avoid stone plates, crystal facets, rock armor, metal armor, jewelry or mechanical paneling. Do not make a cute blob, insect, crab, fish, skull or mechanical cannon. No grinning toothy face, no fangs, no halo, no weapons, no armor costume, no external fog, no particles, no large glowing aura.
Art: beautiful hand-painted dark cosmic game art in exactly the same universe as approved image 1. Strong large-scale shape hierarchy and dramatic readable light/shadow planes. Do not pack the silhouette with tiny spines or noise. Common enemy complexity, not a large boss. Full creature visible, centered with 10 percent empty transparent margins; no ground, cast shadow or background. Genuine alpha transparency, no text.
